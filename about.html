My aim is to bridge the gap between organic chaos and structural precision. I've worked in the creative industry for over 14 years, focused primarily on original fine art and minimalist animation. This background informs the texture-driven, hands-on approach I begin most projects with, while my current training gives me the technical skills to lock these elements into pleasing, functional grids.

I'm in my final year of the Advanced Graphic Design program at Centennial, currently producing multi-page editorial spreads, clean, usable package designs, and hauntingly captivating posters and book covers.
